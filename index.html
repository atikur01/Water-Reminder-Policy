Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 8, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Water Reminder, provided by NexusPlay Engine. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Personal Information

We respect the privacy of our users the highest. We do not collect any information of our users.

Data Retention and Deletion

We believe you should have full control over your data. Since the app works completely offline, we do not transmit or store your data on any remote servers.

  • Data Retention: Your data is retained locally on your device for as long as the application is installed.
  • Data Deletion: You can completely delete all your data at any time. To do this, simply uninstall the application from your device, or clear the app's data and cache in your device's System Settings. Either action will permanently erase all your saved water intake goals, history, and preferences.

Permission Requests

For the app to function properly, the app requires some permissions:

  • Notifications: To send you reminders to drink water.
  • Alarms & Reminders: To schedule exact hydration alerts.
  • Internet: To provide fonts or check for updates. Please note that standard network requests for these resources may expose your IP address to the servers providing them, though we do not store or track this information.
